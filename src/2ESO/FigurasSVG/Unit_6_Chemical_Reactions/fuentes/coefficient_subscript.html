Coefficient (in front) and subscript (small, inside the formula)

✔ We CAN change the coefficient

2H2O
↑ coefficient: 2 molecules↑ subscript: 2 H in each one
H: 2 · 2 = 4 atoms   ·   O: 2 · 1 = 2 atoms
The coefficient multiplies all the atoms of the formula. It is still water.

✘ We can NEVER change the subscripts

H2O2
↑ we changed a subscript!
H2O2 is hydrogen peroxide (the antiseptic for wounds), a different substance.
Changing a subscript changes the substance!
To balance, we only write whole numbers (2, 3, 4…) in front of the formulas: the stoichiometric coefficients. A coefficient of 1 is not written.
