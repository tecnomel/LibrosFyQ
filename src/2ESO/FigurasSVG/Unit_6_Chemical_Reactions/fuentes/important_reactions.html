Important chemical reactions

🔩🟤

Oxidation

4 Fe + 3 O₂ → 2 Fe₂O₃
A substance reacts with the oxygen in the air. Iron rusts, especially with moisture.
🔥

Combustion

fuel + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O + energy
A fast oxidation that releases a lot of heat and light (butane, petrol, wood).
⛏️🏭

Obtaining metals

minerals → metal
Iron from iron oxides in a blast furnace; aluminium from bauxite; copper from chalcopyrite.
🌱☀️

Photosynthesis & respiration

CO₂ + H₂O ⇄ glucose + O₂
Plants store the Sun's energy in glucose; living things release it by breathing.
🍞🧀

Fermentation

sugars → alcohol or acid + CO₂…
Made by yeasts and bacteria: bread, yoghurt, cheese, wine.
💊🍋

Neutralisation

acid + base → salt + water
HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O. Antacids neutralise stomach acid.
