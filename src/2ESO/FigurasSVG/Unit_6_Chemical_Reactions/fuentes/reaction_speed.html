How can we make a reaction faster?

🌡️⬆

Temperature

Hotter → particles move faster → more effective collisions
pressure cooker 🍲 · fridge slows food spoiling 🧊
🧪🧪

Concentration

More particles in the same space → more collisions
vacuum packing removes oxygen
🍖➜🍔

Contact area

Smaller pieces → more surface touching the other reactant
minced meat cooks faster
⚙️✨

Catalysts

Speed up a reaction and are NOT used up
enzymes · car catalytic converter
