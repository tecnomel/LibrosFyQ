Uniform circular motion (UCM)

Period (T): time for one full turn, in s.
Minute hand of a clock: T = 1 h
Frequency (f): turns per second, in Hz.   f = 1 / T
A fan at 300 turns per minute: f = 5 Hz
Linear speed (v): one turn = one circumference, 2πr.   v = 2πr / T
The tip of a long blade moves faster than a point near the centre.
