Free fall: a UARM with a = g ≈ 10 m/s²

v = g · t
Every second, the speed increases by about 10 m/s. The distance fallen each second gets bigger and bigger.
🌙 Hammer and feather
Without air, all objects fall at the same rate, whatever their mass. In 1971, astronaut David Scott dropped a hammer and a feather on the Moon: they hit the ground at the same time!
