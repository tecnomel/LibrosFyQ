How does a wind turbine work?

1The wind has kinetic energy.
2It pushes the blades, which turn: mechanical energy.
3The rotating shaft moves a generator.
4The generator produces electrical energy ⚡.
📍 Castilla-La Mancha is one of the Spanish regions that produce the most wind energy, thanks to its flat land and steady winds. It produces no CO₂, but wind farms change the landscape and can affect birds.
