Properties of energy

🍳🔥

It is transferred

from one body to another
the cooker heats the pan
🔋⛽

It can be stored

to be used later
batteries, fuels, a wound spring
🗼⚡

It is transported

from one place to another
electricity in cables, sunlight through space
🌬️➜⚡

It is transformed

from one form into another
a wind turbine: kinetic → electrical
💡🌡️

It is degraded

part always ends up as useless heat
a bulb gets hot: wasted energy
⚖️

It is conserved

it is never created or destroyed
total energy before = total energy after
