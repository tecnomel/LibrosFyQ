Saving energy every day

⚡ Energy label

A: uses the least energy · G: uses the most
💡Use LED bulbs: they use about 85 % less energy.
🔌Switch off devices: standby also consumes energy.
🌡️Heating at 19–21 °C in winter; close windows when it is on.
🚿Take short showers instead of baths.
🚌🚲Walk, cycle or use public transport.
🏠Good insulation keeps the heat in.
♻️Recycle and buy local products.
🧺Use the washing machine full and at low temperature.
