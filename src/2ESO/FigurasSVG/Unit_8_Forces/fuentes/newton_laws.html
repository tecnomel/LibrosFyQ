Newton's three laws

1st LAW

Inertia

🛑🧍➡️
If no net force acts, an object keeps doing what it was doing: at rest, or moving in a straight line at constant speed.
When a bus brakes, you keep moving forward.
2nd LAW

Force and acceleration

🛒💪
F = m · a
The bigger the force, the bigger the acceleration. The more mass, the harder it is to accelerate.
A full trolley is harder to push than an empty one.
3rd LAW

Action and reaction

⛸️↔️⛸️
If A pushes B, B pushes A with a force of the same size in the opposite direction. They act on different bodies.
Rockets push gas down; the gas pushes the rocket up 🚀
