Separating heterogeneous mixtures

Sieving

solids of very different sizes
pebbles + sand

Filtration

a solid that does not dissolve, from a liquid
sand + water

Decantation

liquids that do not mix (different density)
oil + water

Magnetic separation

iron and other magnetic metals
iron filings + sawdust

Solvent extraction

a solvent that dissolves only ONE component
sand + salt (use water)

Centrifugation

it spins very fast: the denser part goes to the bottom
blood · spin-dryer
