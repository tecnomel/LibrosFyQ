Physical or chemical change?

Physical change

Same substance before and after (same formula)
🧊➜💧changes of state
🛤️☀️expansion
🍯❄️temperature (honey thickens)
🧂+💧mixing / dissolving

Chemical change

NEW substances appear (different formula)
🪵🔥burning wood
🔩🟤a nail rusting
🍳frying an egg
🍞baking bread

Clues that a chemical change is happening 🔍

🎨Colour changea nail turns brown
🫧Gas bubblesvinegar + baking soda
🧪Precipitatea solid appears in a liquid
🌡️Temperature changeit gets hot or cold
💡Lightfireworks, a candle
