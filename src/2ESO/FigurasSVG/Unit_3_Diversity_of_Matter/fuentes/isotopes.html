Isotopes: same element, different number of neutrons

Carbon-12   126C

6 protons · 6 neutrons · 6 electrons

Carbon-14   146C

6 protons · 8 neutrons · 6 electrons
Same Z = 6 → both are carbon  ·  Different A → different mass
Carbon-14 is radioactive: it is used to find out the age of fossils and ancient remains 🦴
