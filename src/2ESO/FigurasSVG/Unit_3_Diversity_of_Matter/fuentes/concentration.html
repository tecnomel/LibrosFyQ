How much solute is there? Concentration

Concentration in g/L

C = mass of solute (g)volume of solution (L)
grams of solute in each litre of solution
🧂💧
15 g of salt → 0.5 L of solution
C = 15 g0.5 L = 30 g/L

Percentage by mass

% mass = mass of solute (g)mass of solution (g) · 100
grams of solute in each 100 g of solution
🍬🫖
10 g of sugar + 90 g of water = 100 g
% = 10 g100 g · 100 = 10 %

Percentage by volume

% vol = volume of solutevolume of solution · 100
mL of solute in each 100 mL of solution
🧪💧
12 mL of alcohol + 88 mL of water = 100 mL
% = 12 mL100 mL · 100 = 12 %
⚠ Below the line there is always the SOLUTION, not the solvent: solution = solute(s) + solvent
