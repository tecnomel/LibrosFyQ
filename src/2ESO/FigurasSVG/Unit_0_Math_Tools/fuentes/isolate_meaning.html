What does “isolating” a quantity mean?

To isolate a quantity (to “clear” it, despejar in Spanish) means to leave it completely alone on one side of the equals sign:
✔ with nothing added, subtracted, multiplied or divided next to it,
✔ in the numerator (on top), never below the fraction line,
✔ and with everything else on the other side.
x = 4✔ isolated
2x = 8✘ it is multiplied by 2
x + 3 = 7✘ it has a 3 added
1x = 5✘ it is below the line
