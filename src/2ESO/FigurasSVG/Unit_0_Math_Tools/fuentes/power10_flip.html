Moving a power of 10 up or down: the sign of the exponent changes

From the bottom to the top

6103⟶6 · 10-3
103 goes up: its exponent changes from +3 to −3.

From the top to the bottom

4 · 10-2⟶4102
10-2 goes down: its exponent changes from −2 to +2.

Useful to divide

10510-2⟶105 · 102 = 107
Move it up, then add the exponents.
Only the power of 10 moves and changes the sign of its exponent. The number in front stays where it is.
