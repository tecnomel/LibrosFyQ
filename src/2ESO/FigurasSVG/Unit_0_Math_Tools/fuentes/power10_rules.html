The four rules of powers of 10

1. Multiplying: ADD the exponents

10a · 10b = 10a + b
103 · 104 = 103+4 = 107  ·  105 · 10-2 = 105+(-2) = 103
With numbers: multiply the numbers and add the exponents.

2. Dividing: SUBTRACT the exponents

10a / 10b = 10a − b
107 / 103 = 107−3 = 104  ·  102 / 10-3 = 102−(−3) = 105
top exponent minus bottom exponent

3. Moving up or down: CHANGE the sign

110b = 10−b
1103 = 10-3  ·  510-2 = 5 · 102
A power of 10 can go up or down the fraction line if its exponent changes sign.

4. Power of a power: MULTIPLY the exponents

(10a)b = 10a · b
(102)3 = 102·3 = 106  ·  (10-3)2 = 10(−3)·2 = 10-6
Used to change units of surface (²) and volume (³).
