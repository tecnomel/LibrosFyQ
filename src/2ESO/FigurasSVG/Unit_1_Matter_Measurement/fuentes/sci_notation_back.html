From scientific notation to decimal: the exponent tells you the jumps

Positive exponent

2,7 · 103
+3 → 3 jumps to the RIGHT (the number gets bigger)
→
2700
bigger than 1

Negative exponent

4,6 · 10−2
−2 → 2 jumps to the LEFT (the number gets smaller)
→
0,046
smaller than 1
0 = a zero we add when there are no more digits to jump over.
