The scientific method

1
👀
Observation
We notice something and ask a question.
➜
2
💡
Hypothesis
A possible explanation we can test.
➜
3
🧪
Experiment
Change ONE variable and measure.
➜
4
📊
Analysis
Organise data in tables and graphs.
➜
5
✅
Conclusion
Accept or reject the hypothesis.
✗ Hypothesis rejected? Propose a new one and test it again!
📢 Finally, scientists communicate their results using scientific language and SI units.
