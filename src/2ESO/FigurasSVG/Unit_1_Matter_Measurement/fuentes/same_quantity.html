We can only change between units of the SAME quantity

✔ YES: same quantity

Length
km⇄m⇄cm
multiples and
submultiples
Mass
kg⇄g⇄mg
multiples and
submultiples
Time
h⇄min⇄s
different units,
same quantity
Volume
dm³⇄L⇄mL
different units,
same quantity

✘ NO: different quantities

km✘kg
length ≠ mass
s✘m
time ≠ length
L✘g
capacity ≠ mass
m²✘m³
surface ≠ volume
Before changing a unit, ask yourself: “Do both units measure the same quantity?” If the answer is no, the change is impossible.
