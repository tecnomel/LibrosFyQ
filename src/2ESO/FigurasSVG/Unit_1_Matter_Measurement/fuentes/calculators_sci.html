How to type 3.2 · 10-3 on each scientific calculator

EXP and ×10x are the same key: it means “× 10 to the power of”. Never type “× 1 0” before it!
The minus sign of the exponent: +/− goes after the number (old models); (−) goes before it (from 1997).
Simplified drawings: the position of the keys can change from one model to another.
