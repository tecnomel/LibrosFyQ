Anatomy of a conversion factor

① Starting quantity: the number and the unit we want to change.
② Numerator: the new unit we want to obtain.
③ The "1" always goes with the bigger unit: 1 km = 103 m.
2.5 km· 103 m1 km =2.5 · 103 m = 2500 m
④ Denominator: the old unit, so that it cancels out with the starting unit.
Top and bottom are the same amount, so the factor is worth exactly 1:
the quantity does not change, only its unit.
