Matter is divided into bodies and material systems

MATTEReverything that has mass and occupies space

Body

A portion of matter with its own shape and defined boundaries: we can see where it begins and where it ends.
⚽✏️🪑🌕
ball, pencil, chair, Moon

Material system

A portion of matter with no shape of its own and no defined boundaries: it takes the shape of its container.
🥛🌬️🧃⛽
milk, air, juice, petrol
