How to read a unit: from right to left

1
Find the symbol of the unit: it is the last letter (or letters).
2
Say which quantity it measures: m → length, g → mass, L → capacity, s → time…
3
Look at the letter in front: it is the prefix. Multiple or submultiple?
km
↓↓
m = metre
→ length
k = kilo = 103
MULTIPLE
mg
↓↓
g = gram
→ mass
m = milli = 10-3
SUBMULTIPLE
cL
↓↓
L = litre
→ capacity
c = centi = 10-2
SUBMULTIPLE
mm
↓↓
m (2nd) = metre
→ length
m (1st) = milli = 10-3
SUBMULTIPLE
hPa
↓↓
Pa = pascal
→ pressure
h = hecto = 102
MULTIPLE
If there is no letter in front of the unit (m, g, L, s…), it is the base unit. Careful with mm: the first m is the prefix and the second m is the unit!
