How to isolate a quantity in d = m / V

An equation is like a balanced scale: the two sides are equal.
If we do the same operation on both sides (multiply or divide by the same thing),
the scale stays balanced. We use this to leave the quantity we want alone.

I want the mass, m

1
Write the formula.
d = mV
2
V is dividing m: multiply both sides by V.
d · V = m · VV
3
V/V = 1, so it cancels. Turn it round.
m = d · V

I want the volume, V

1
Start from m = d · V (steps on the left).
m = d · V
2
d is multiplying V: divide both sides by d.
md = d · Vd
3
d/d = 1, so it cancels. Turn it round.
V = md
Shortcut: what is dividing on one side goes to the other side multiplying, and what is multiplying goes to the other side dividing.
