The boiling point of water depends on pressure

🏔️

Top of Everest

p ≈ 0.33 atm
≈ 70 °C
Pasta never cooks properly!
🏖️

Sea level

p = 1 atm
100 °C
The usual boiling point.
🍲

Pressure cooker

p ≈ 2 atm
≈ 120 °C
Food cooks much faster.
More pressure ➜ higher boiling point  ·  Less pressure ➜ lower boiling point
