Evaporation or boiling?

Evaporation

  • Only at the surface of the liquid.
  • At any temperature (slow).
  • Faster with heat ☀️, wind 🌬️ and a large surface.

Boiling

  • In the whole liquid: bubbles form inside.
  • Only at the boiling point (100 °C for water).
  • Fast and with lots of bubbles 🫧.
