Water in the weather: which change of state?

☁️

Clouds

condensation
vapour cools high up and forms tiny drops
🌧️

Rain

no change
drops in clouds grow and fall
🌫️

Mist / fog

condensation
a cloud close to the ground
🌨️

Snow

freezing / desublimation
tiny ice crystals join into snowflakes
🧊

Hail

freezing
drops freeze while winds push them up and down
🍃💧

Dew

condensation
vapour turns into drops on cold surfaces at night
❄️🍂

Frost

desublimation
vapour turns directly into ice on very cold surfaces
✈️

Contrails

desublimation
vapour from engines turns into ice crystals
All of them are physical changes: the substance is always water, H2O.
