The gas laws

Boyle-Mariotte

T constant 🌡️
p · V = constant
Volume ⬇  →  pressure ⬆
inversely proportional

Gay-Lussac

V constant 📦
p / T = constant
Temperature ⬆  →  pressure ⬆
directly proportional

Charles

p constant 🎈
V / T = constant
Temperature ⬆  →  volume ⬆
directly proportional
If p, V and T all change: general gas law   p1·V1T1 = p2·V2T2
