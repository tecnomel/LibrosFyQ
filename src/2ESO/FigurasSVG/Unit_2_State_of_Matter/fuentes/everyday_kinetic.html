Kinetic theory explains everyday life

🌸💨

Diffusion

The smell of perfume spreads through the whole room.

Gas particles move freely and mix with the air.
💉

Compressibility

We can squeeze the air in a closed syringe, but not water.

Gas particles have lots of empty space between them.
🛤️☀️

Thermal expansion

Railway tracks and bridges have small gaps between sections.

When heated, particles vibrate more and move slightly apart.
🧴🔥

Pressure and temperature

Aerosol cans must never be heated: they can explode.

Hot gas particles hit the walls faster and harder.
